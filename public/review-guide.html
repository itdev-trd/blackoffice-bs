Besight Backend — testing guide for App Review

Internal customer-messaging tool of Traderider Media Group. This page explains how to test each requested permission. The app interface is in Thai, so every button is shown here with its English meaning.

What this app is

We run Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts for our own brand (BeSight). Customers click our Click-to-Messenger ads and message the Page. This app is the internal console our own staff use to read those conversations and answer them. It is not distributed to third parties and has no public sign-up: accounts are created by us for our own employees.

Permission: pages_messaging

Why we need it — to read the messages customers send to our Page and to reply to them from this console, inside the 24-hour standard messaging window (we send with messaging_type: RESPONSE, and HUMAN_AGENT for follow-ups our staff write by hand). We also subscribe to the messages, message_echoes and message_reads webhooks so the read state in this console matches the Page inbox and two staff members do not answer the same customer twice.

Steps to reproduce

  1. Sign in with the test account.
  2. In the left menu, click ตอบแชท (Inbox).
  3. The list on the left shows conversations. Tabs above it are ทั้งหมด (All), Messenger, Instagram, LINE OA. Click the Messenger tab, then click any conversation.
  4. The conversation opens in the middle. The customer's messages are on the left, ours on the right, each one labelled with the staff member who sent it.
  5. Type a message in the box at the bottom — placeholder พิมพ์คำตอบเป็นไทย... (type your reply) — and press ส่ง (Send).
  6. The message is delivered to the customer on Messenger and appears in the thread with a timestamp.

Permission: instagram_manage_messages

Why we need it — the same console also answers Instagram direct messages for the Instagram professional account connected to the same Page. Customers reach us there from our Instagram ads.

Steps to reproduce — same as above, but click the Instagram tab in step 3. Open a thread, type a reply, press ส่ง (Send).

Permission: instagram_manage_comments

Why we need it — customers ask questions in the comments under our ads. Staff answer them from the ฟีด (คอมเมนต์) (Feed / comments) page, either as a public reply under the comment or as a one-time private reply, which is how we move a public question into a private conversation.

Steps to reproduce

  1. In the left menu, click ฟีด (คอมเมนต์) (Feed / comments).
  2. Each card is one comment. Type in the box inside the card and press ส่ง (Send) to publish the reply under that comment.
  3. Ticking ส่งเป็นข้อความส่วนตัว (DM) แทน sends it as a private reply instead.

You can complete the whole loop yourself

No special setup is needed. From your own Facebook account, send a message to our Page BeSight. Your message arrives in this console within a few seconds. Open it, type a reply, press ส่ง (Send) — the reply arrives in Messenger on your own device.

That single loop exercises pages_messaging end to end: the messages webhook that delivers your message, the console our staff read it in, and the Send API call that answers it. The same works for Instagram — message the Instagram professional account linked to the same Page, then answer from the Instagram tab.

Replies are sent with messaging_type: RESPONSE inside the standard 24-hour window. If you come back to a conversation more than 24 hours later, the console will tell you the window has closed instead of silently failing.
