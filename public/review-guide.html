App Besight — testing guide for App Review

Internal customer-messaging tool of Traderider Media Group. This page explains how to test each requested permission. The app interface is in Thai, so every button is shown here with its English meaning.

What this app is

We run Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts for our own brand (BeSight). Customers click our Click-to-Messenger ads and message the Page. This app is the internal console our own staff use to read those conversations and answer them. It is not distributed to third parties and has no public sign-up: accounts are created by us for our own employees.

Permission: pages_messaging

Why we need it — to read the messages customers send to our Page and to reply to them from this console, inside the 24-hour standard messaging window (we send with messaging_type: RESPONSE, and HUMAN_AGENT for follow-ups our staff write by hand). We also subscribe to the messages, message_echoes and message_reads webhooks so the read state in this console matches the Page inbox and two staff members do not answer the same customer twice.

Steps to reproduce

  1. Sign in with the test account.
  2. In the left menu, click ตอบแชท (Inbox).
  3. The list on the left shows conversations. Tabs above it are ทั้งหมด (All), Messenger, Instagram, LINE OA. Click the Messenger tab, then click any conversation.
  4. The conversation opens in the middle. The customer's messages are on the left, ours on the right, each one labelled with the staff member who sent it.
  5. Type a message in the box at the bottom — placeholder พิมพ์คำตอบเป็นไทย... (type your reply) — and press ส่ง (Send).
  6. The message is delivered to the customer on Messenger and appears in the thread with a timestamp.

Permission: instagram_manage_messages

Why we need it — the same console also answers Instagram direct messages for the Instagram professional account connected to the same Page. Customers reach us there from our Instagram ads.

Steps to reproduce — same as above, but click the Instagram tab in step 3. Open a thread, type a reply, press ส่ง (Send).

Permission: instagram_manage_comments

Why we need it — customers ask questions in the comments under our ads. Staff answer them from the ฟีด (คอมเมนต์) (Feed / comments) page, either as a public reply under the comment or as a one-time private reply, which is how we move a public question into a private conversation.

Steps to reproduce

  1. In the left menu, click ฟีด (คอมเมนต์) (Feed / comments).
  2. Each card is one comment. Type in the box inside the card and press ส่ง (Send) to publish the reply under that comment.
  3. Ticking ส่งเป็นข้อความส่วนตัว (DM) แทน sends it as a private reply instead.

Permission: pages_manage_metadata (custom labels)

Why we need it — staff tag each Messenger conversation with our own labels (for example “account opened”, “new customer”) so the same tags show in the Page inbox in Meta Business Suite. Labels are created and attached through the Page’s custom_labels endpoints. We only label people who messaged our own Page.

Steps to reproduce

  1. Open any Messenger conversation in ตอบแชท (Inbox).
  2. In the right-hand panel, find ป้ายกำกับ (ซิงก์กับ Meta) (Labels — synced with Meta).
  3. Click a label button, e.g. ✅ เปิดบัญชีแล้ว (Account opened). The label is attached to that person in the Page inbox. Click it again to remove it.

Permission: page_events (Conversions API for Business Messaging)

Why we need it — when a conversation that came from one of our Click-to-Messenger ads turns into a qualified lead, staff mark its stage. We send that stage to the Page’s dataset as a Conversions API event (action_source: business_messaging, identified only by the Page-scoped ID) so Meta can measure which of our ads produce real leads. No email, phone or other personal data is sent.

Steps to reproduce

  1. Open a Messenger conversation in ตอบแชท (Inbox).
  2. In the right-hand panel, under ระยะข้อมูลลูกค้า (Lead stage), click มีคุณสมบัติ (Qualified) or สร้างคอนเวอร์ชั่นแล้ว (Converted).
  3. The stage is saved and the event is sent to the Page’s dataset. If sending fails, the reason is shown in red under the buttons.

Permissions: pages_read_user_content, pages_manage_engagement

Why we need them — customers ask questions in the comments under our own Page posts and ads. pages_read_user_content lets the console list those comments; pages_manage_engagement lets staff publish a reply under the comment as the Page. We only read and reply to comments on our own Page.

Steps to reproduce

  1. Open blackoffice-bs.vercel.app/feed (ฟีด (คอมเมนต์), Feed / comments).
  2. Each card is one comment on our Page. Type in the box inside the card and press ส่ง (Send). The reply is published under that comment on Facebook.

Feature: Human Agent

Why we need it — our staff are real people who answer questions that often need checking (for example account opening status). When a customer's last message is older than 24 hours, the console sends the staff member's hand-written reply with the HUMAN_AGENT tag, within 7 days of the customer's last message. Every such message is typed by a person; nothing is automated or promotional.

Steps to reproduce — open a Messenger conversation whose last customer message is between 24 hours and 7 days old, type a reply and press ส่ง (Send).

Permissions: pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, instagram_basic

Used to list the Page and the linked Instagram professional account our staff manage, and to show the Page and Instagram account name and picture at the top of the Inbox (BeSight selector above the conversation list).

Permissions: ads_read, ads_management, business_management

Why we need them — the same console is where our marketing staff run our own ad accounts (owned by our business, Traderider Media Group).

  • ads_read — the แคมเปญ (Campaigns) and วิเคราะห์ (Analyze) pages show spend, results and cost per message for our campaigns, ad sets and ads, and แอดไหนได้ลูกค้า (Which ad brought customers) joins conversations to the ad they came from.
  • ads_management — on the Campaigns page an admin can pause or resume a campaign, ad set or ad with the switch in its row (a confirmation dialog appears first). Nothing changes automatically without an admin confirming it.
  • business_management — to list the ad accounts and Pages that belong to our business portfolio.

These screens control real ad spend, so the reviewer account cannot open them. The attached screencasts show each step on our live ad accounts.

You can complete the whole loop yourself

No special setup is needed. From your own Facebook account, send a message to our Page BeSight. Your message usually arrives in this console within a minute (at most about two minutes). Open it, type a reply, press ส่ง (Send) — the reply arrives in Messenger on your own device.

That single loop exercises pages_messaging end to end: the messages webhook that delivers your message, the console our staff read it in, and the Send API call that answers it. The same works for Instagram — message the Instagram professional account linked to the same Page, then answer from the Instagram tab.

Replies are sent with messaging_type: RESPONSE inside the standard 24-hour window. If you come back to a conversation more than 24 hours later, the console will tell you the window has closed instead of silently failing.
